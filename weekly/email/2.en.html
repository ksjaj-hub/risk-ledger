New facts this week

Inflation, now on the Fed card.
The PCE price index rose 3.8% in the year to October and 0.3% on the month. Excluding food and energy it rose 3.3%. September was 3.6%. The card's conditions count consumer prices (CPI), so this figure does not change the count. BEA release, November 25

What bond prices show.
The 2-year Treasury yield was 0.63 points above the top of the Fed's range on November 24. Above zero means bond prices show rate rises ahead. The inflation priced into 5-year Treasuries was 2.13% on November 25, in the lowest tenth of its last five years. Short-term rate risk shows in prices. Lasting inflation does not. Method and sources
