The CEO came back. The inquiry did not go away.

All 12 cards are open. None got worse, eased or was resolved this week.

GOOD NEWS THAT LEFT THE RISK IN PLACE
- Nvidia's CEO returned on November 23. Still open: the government investigations and inquiries the company itself refers to.
- Dell beat forecasts on November 24. Its shares fell about 7% the next day. Still open: whether the debt-funded AI buildout pays for itself.
- Volatility fell. VIX went from 35.89 on November 20 to 26.36 on November 24. That is a reading, not a resolution. Still open: the tariff proposal, the rate path and the oil shock.
