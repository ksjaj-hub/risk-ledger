New facts this week

Tariffs.
On November 25 the US Trade Representative proposed tariffs on 14 economies, with ceilings from 15% to 35%. Comments close on January 5, 2027. No effective date is set. USTR release

Inflation.
The PCE price index rose 3.8% in the year to October, up from 3.6% in September. Excluding food and energy it rose 3.3%. BEA release, November 25
