What this is and is not

AFMX describes risk conditions using a published rule. This week the rule was run over data from 1990, its weak points were published, and it now stands at version 3 with five steps. The conditions on each card were written before the outcomes were known, and the record cannot be edited afterwards. Nothing here is advice to buy or sell anything.

30~39점에서 과거에 온 것

2007년 이후 점수가 30~39점이던 날은 487거래일입니다. 그날들 뒤 석 달 동안, 가장 낮게 마감한 날에 시장이 얼마나 내려가 있었는지입니다.

  • 5% 안쪽69%
  • 5~10% 조정18%
  • 10~20% 하락13%
  • 20% 넘게 하락1% 미만

과거에 있었던 횟수이며 예측이 아닙니다. 실제와 맞았는지는 기록이 12개월 쌓인 뒤 처음 공개 점검합니다.
