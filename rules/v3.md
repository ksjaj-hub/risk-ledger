# Judging rule v3 (US market)

Effective from 2026-11-26. The machine-readable version is `v3.json`; if the two ever disagree, `v3.json` is what ran.
Records made under v1 and v2 keep their version. Earlier rule files are unchanged.

## What changed from v2, and why

The owner asked for a finer scale than four states. Five and seven states were compared on the same
past data as before (see `backtest/`). v3 changes one thing:

**Five states instead of four.** A new state, **Guarded**, sits between Calm and Watch, and Watch now
starts at a score of 30 instead of 25. Elevated and Stress are exactly as in v2.

| Score | v2 | v3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 14 | Calm | Calm |
| 15 to 24 | Calm | Guarded |
| 25 to 29 | Watch | Guarded |
| 30 to 49 | Watch | Watch |
| 50 to 74 | Elevated | Elevated |
| 75 to 100 | Stress | Stress |

Why five and not seven: the score can take only about twenty different values. Cut into seven, two
neighbouring states were followed by the same outcomes, and the top states were almost never reached.

Why the top was left alone: a version that lowered Stress to a score of 67 was also tried. It made
Stress and Elevated indistinguishable in what followed and added 58 Stress days in 2023, so it was dropped.

What the past data showed for v3 (9,352 days from 1990; share of days in each state that were followed,
within 60 trading days, by a VIX close of 30 or more):

| State | Share of days | Followed by VIX of 30 or more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm | 39% | 8% |
| Guarded | 21% | 27% |
| Watch | 21% | 36% |
| Elevated | 16% | 69% |
| Stress | 3% | 88% |

The cost: the published state changed about 11.5 times a year on past data, against 7.4 under v2.
The step between Guarded and Watch is the weakest of the four.

This comparison is hindsight on revised data, and before late 2016 only three of the five components
were available. The ledger from 2026-11-26 onward is the real test.

## What the rule does

It describes the current risk condition of the US stock market using five components. It does not
predict prices.

Each component gets a level from 0 to 3: **Low, Moderate, High, Extreme**. Thresholds are the same as v1 and v2.

| Component | Input | Low | Moderate | High | Extreme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equity volatility | VIX close | below 15 | 15 to 20 | 20 to 30 | 30 or more |
| Credit stress | US high-yield bond spread (percentage points) | below 3.5 | 3.5 to 5 | 5 to 7 | 7 or more |
| Yield curve | 10-year minus 3-month Treasury yield | 0.5 or more | 0 to 0.5 | -0.5 to 0 | below -0.5 |
| Financial conditions | Chicago Fed NFCI | below -0.5 | -0.5 to 0 | 0 to 0.5 | 0.5 or more |
| Equity drawdown | S&P 500 close, % below its highest close of the last 252 trading days | below 5 | 5 to 10 | 10 to 20 | 20 or more |

A value exactly on a boundary belongs to the higher-risk level.

## Score and indicated state

Score = 100 x (sum of the five levels) / 15, rounded.

| Score | Indicated state |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 14 | Calm |
| 15 to 29 | Guarded |
| 30 to 49 | Watch |
| 50 to 74 | Elevated |
| 75 to 100 | Stress |

Floors: if any one component **other than the yield curve** is Extreme, the indicated state is at least
Elevated. If two or more of them are Extreme, it is Stress.

## Published state

- If the indicated state is higher than the published state, the published state moves up the same day.
- If the indicated state has been lower than the published state for 5 trading days in a row, the
  published state moves down to the highest state indicated in those 5 days.
- Otherwise it stays.

A trading day is a date on which a daily market input (VIX, high-yield spread, yield curve) has an
observation. The first v3 record starts from the last state published under v2.

Each record shows both: `state` (published) and `indicated_state` (what the rule said that day), plus
the indicated state of the last 5 trading days, so the confirmation step can be checked by hand.

## Alerts

An alert is recorded whenever the published state changes or any component changes level, compared
with the previous record.

## Known limits of v3

- The thresholds and the score bands are round numbers chosen by judgment and then checked on past data.
- The state describes current conditions. In the backtest it reacted after declines had begun, not before.
- More states means more state changes. Guarded and Watch differ less than the other neighbours.
- The backtest could use the S&P 500 only from late 2016 and the high-yield spread only from late 2023
  (source limits), so 2008 was judged on three components and 2020 and 2022 on four.
- Holding a state for 5 days means the published state can stay higher than the day's indicated state.
- Inputs lag by about one trading day; NFCI is weekly.
- No event information feeds the score; events are tracked separately in `events/`.

Any change to the above will be published as v4. This file will not be edited.
