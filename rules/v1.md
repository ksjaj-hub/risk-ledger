# Judging rule v1 (US market)

Effective from 2026-11-26. The machine-readable version is `v1.json`; if the two ever disagree, `v1.json` is what ran.

## What the rule does

It describes the current risk condition of the US stock market using five components. It does not
predict prices.

Each component gets a level from 0 to 3: **Low, Moderate, High, Extreme**.

| Component | Input | Low | Moderate | High | Extreme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Equity volatility | VIX close | below 15 | 15 to 20 | 20 to 30 | 30 or more |
| Credit stress | US high-yield bond spread (percentage points) | below 3.5 | 3.5 to 5 | 5 to 7 | 7 or more |
| Yield curve | 10-year minus 3-month Treasury yield | 0.5 or more | 0 to 0.5 | -0.5 to 0 | below -0.5 |
| Financial conditions | Chicago Fed NFCI | below -0.5 | -0.5 to 0 | 0 to 0.5 | 0.5 or more |
| Equity drawdown | S&P 500 close, % below its highest close of the last 252 trading days | below 5 | 5 to 10 | 10 to 20 | 20 or more |

A value exactly on a boundary belongs to the higher-risk level.

## Score and state

Score = 100 x (sum of the five levels) / 15, rounded.

| Score | State |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 24 | Calm |
| 25 to 49 | Watch |
| 50 to 74 | Elevated |
| 75 to 100 | Stress |

Floors: if any one component is Extreme, the state is at least Elevated. If two or more are Extreme, the state is Stress.

## Alerts

An alert is recorded whenever the state changes or any component changes level, compared with the previous record.

## Known limits of v1

- The thresholds are round, conventional numbers chosen by judgment. They have not been tuned against history.
- There is no smoothing, so a value hovering at a boundary can produce repeated alerts.
- Inputs lag by about one trading day; NFCI is weekly.
- Five components, equal weight. No event information feeds the score; events are tracked separately in `events/`.

Any change to the above will be published as v2. This file will not be edited.
